Ag-Tech Roots

Our Story

Big Data in a Box (BDiB) was founded on a simple insight: small and medium-sized organic farmers spend too much valuable time on paperwork. The inspiration occurred when CEO Dr. Sree Nilakanta listened to an NPR broadcast highlighting the frustrations of organic growers undergoing seed-to-sale certification audits.

"Achieving compliance and record-keeping shouldn't keep you from focusing on your crops. We wanted to build tools that return desk hours back to field hours." Dr. Sree Nilakanta, CEO & Founder

Leveraging programs at the Iowa State University (ISU) Pappajohn Entrepreneurship Center, the founding team pivoted from hardware storage modules into building SPIDER Ag—an intelligent, cloud-backed voice and OCR tracking system built in Ames, Iowa.
